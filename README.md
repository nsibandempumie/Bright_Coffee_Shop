☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
Project Overview

This project analyzes Bright Coffee Shop sales data to uncover business insights, evaluate sales performance, and provide data-driven recommendations for management. The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, from data exploration and transformation to visualization and executive reporting.

Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

Analyze overall sales performance.
Identify the best-performing store locations.
Determine the top-selling product categories and products.
Analyze sales trends over time.
Identify peak trading periods.
Present actionable business recommendations through an interactive dashboard.
Tools Used
Databricks SQL – Data exploration (EDA) and ETL
Power BI – Dashboard development
Google Sheets – Pivot tables and charts
Canva – Flowchart and executive presentation
Project Workflow
1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The dataset was explored to:

Check for missing values
Identify duplicate records
Validate data types
Generate summary statistics
Understand sales distribution
Explore product, store, and time-based performance
2. ETL Process

The dataset was transformed by:

Creating a cleaned dataset
Renaming columns for consistency
Calculating Revenue
Calculating Gross Profit
Calculating Unit Price
Calculating Cost per Unit
Calculating Gross Profit Percentage
Creating a Gold table with additional business metrics and date dimensions
3. Dashboard Development

An executive dashboard was created to visualize key business metrics, including:

Total Revenue
Total Transactions
Total Units Sold
Number of Stores
Monthly Revenue Trend
Revenue by Store
Revenue by Product Category

Interactive visuals allow management to quickly monitor sales performance and identify trends.

Key Insights
Coffee products generated the highest revenue.
Hell's Kitchen achieved the highest revenue among the three store locations.
Sales were strongest during the peak trading months.
Revenue was concentrated among a small number of high-performing product categories.
Sales trends indicate seasonal demand patterns.
Business Recommendations
Increase inventory levels for high-demand coffee products.
Apply successful operational strategies from the Hell's Kitchen branch to other stores.
Introduce targeted promotions for lower-performing product categories.
Improve inventory planning based on monthly demand trends.
Continue monitoring performance through interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.
